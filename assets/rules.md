# Push Fight Official Rules

## 1. Game Overview and Components
Push Fight is a tactical board game for two players where the objective is to push one of your opponent's pieces off either end of the board.

### The Game Board
The board is an irregular grid. The two far ends are open scoring zones. The longitudinal sides feature thick "Side Rails" which act as impassable walls. Pieces cannot be pushed through these rails.

### Game Pieces
Each player controls 5 pieces:
- 3 Square Pieces (Kings): The only pieces capable of initiating a Push.
- 2 Round Pieces (Pawns): Agile pieces that can move but cannot initiate a Push.

### The Anchor
The Anchor is a neutral marker used to track the last piece that performed a push. It restricts the next player's movement and pushing options to prevent repetitive gameplay.

## 2. Setup and Initial Placement
Unlike many board games, Push Fight begins with an empty board and a strategic placement phase.

### Placement Phase
- Player A places all 5 of their pieces anywhere on their designated half of the board.
- Player B then places all 5 of their pieces anywhere on their remaining half of the board.

### Starting the Game
Player A takes the first turn. The Anchor is not placed on the board until the first player completes their very first push.

## 3. Movement Rules
Movement is the first phase of every turn. It is optional but must occur before the mandatory push.

### Movement Allowance
Each turn, a player may make up to two moves. A player can choose to move zero, one, or two times.

### Movement Action
A single move consists of sliding one piece (Square or Round) any number of empty squares in a straight line orthogonally (horizontally or vertically). 

### Movement Restrictions
- You cannot move diagonally.
- You cannot move through or jump over other pieces.
- You can move the same piece twice or two different pieces once.
- You cannot move a piece that is currently held by the opponent’s Anchor.

## 4. Pushing Mechanics
The push is the second phase of every turn and is the primary way to interact with the opponent.

### Mandatory Push
After the movement phase, a player must perform exactly one push. If a player is unable to perform a legal push, they immediately lose the game.

### How to Push
A push is executed by moving a Square Piece one square into an adjacent space occupied by another piece (friendly or enemy). All pieces in a continuous straight line behind the target piece are shifted one square in that direction.

### Pushing Restrictions
- Only Square pieces can initiate a push. Round pieces are passive.
- Pushing occurs only in the four cardinal directions (no diagonals).
- You cannot push a line of pieces if any piece in that line would hit a Side Rail.
- You cannot push into, or move a line of pieces that includes, the piece currently marked by the Anchor.

## 5. The Anchor and Turn Resolution
The Anchor is a critical mechanic for controlling the board and limiting the opponent's immediate responses.

### Placement of the Anchor
At the very end of a turn, after the push is completed, the player must place the Anchor on top of the Square piece used to perform that push.

### Effect of the Anchor
On the opponent's immediate next turn:
1. The opponent cannot move the anchored piece during their movement phase.
2. The opponent cannot push the anchored piece. It acts as a fixed, immovable wall.

### Anchor Duration
The Anchor remains on the piece until the current player completes their own push phase, at which point it is transferred to the new pushing piece.

## 6. Winning and Losing Conditions
The game ends instantly when a piece leaves the playing surface at either of the two open ends.

### Scoring a Win
A player wins if they push one of their opponent's pieces off the board.

### Losing by Fault
A player loses if they push one of their own pieces off the board. Even if an opponent's piece is also pushed off in the same move, the player who initiated the push is the loser.

### Victory by Trap
If a player begins their turn and is unable to complete a legal push (due to being boxed in by the Anchor or Side Rails), they immediately lose the game.
